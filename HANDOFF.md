- Version 1.2.16 fixes the `model-audit` skill after an independent audit of 1.2.15 by GPT-6.2 Sol and Claude Fable 5.2 (high), and after qualifying it on a second Mac. The process scanner (`boxcheck.c`) no longer refuses every launch on a Mac with an open Terminal window: it reads a root-owned `login` process's owner and start time from the kernel's process table, and only a process of this user started during the attempt that still cannot be read is unclassifiable. The skill now states its known limits up front, restores the rule never to run anything from a reviewer's folders after a run, restores the `.agent` pack exclusion, keeps user-ordered stages, says how other agents use the skill folder, and gives a read-only fallback through the `codex` and `gemini` skills where it cannot run.
